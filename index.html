Nutrition Basics

Macronutrients

Carbs fuel activity, proteins build and repair tissues, and healthy fats support hormones and brain function.

Micronutrients

Vitamins and minerals support immunity, bones, blood health, and cellular processes, even in small amounts.

Hydration

Adequate daily water helps digestion, temperature control, focus, and workout recovery.

How To Read A Food Label

  • Serving size first: all numbers depend on this amount.
  • Check calories: compare calories per serving with your goals.
  • Watch sodium, sugar, and saturated fat: lower is usually better for frequent foods.
  • Ingredient list: shorter, recognizable ingredients can be a better signal.

If Intake Is Too Low: Risks And Solutions

Low intake of protein, vitamins, or water can affect energy, immunity, recovery, and focus. These are practical first-step actions you can take.

Low Protein

  • Possible issues: weak muscle recovery, low satiety, fatigue, slower healing.
  • Simple fixes: include eggs, lentils, yogurt, tofu, or chicken in 2 meals/day.
  • Starter target: spread protein across meals, not only at dinner.

Low Vitamins

  • Possible issues: poor immunity, low energy, skin/hair changes, weak concentration.
  • Simple fixes: eat 4-5 colors of fruits/vegetables each day.
  • Starter target: half your lunch and dinner plate from plant foods.

Low Water

  • Possible issues: headaches, constipation, low focus, lower workout performance.
  • Simple fixes: carry a bottle, set reminders, and pair water with meals.
  • Starter target: begin with 6-8 glasses, then adjust by activity and weather.

If symptoms persist, seek guidance from a qualified healthcare professional.
